# Estimation and Capacity Planning

## Learn
- Estimate requests per second from user and activity assumptions.
- Estimate storage growth, bandwidth, and hot-key concentration.
- Distinguish average load from peaks and understand headroom.
- Use rough orders of magnitude to compare options, then validate with measurements.

## Practice
Estimate daily writes, retained storage, and peak reads for a service. Show the assumptions and recalculate after changing user count or retention.

## Ready when
Another engineer can follow your estimates and identify which assumptions dominate them.
