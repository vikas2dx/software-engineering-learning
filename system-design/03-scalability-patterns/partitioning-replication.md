# Partitioning and Replication

## Learn
- Replicas for availability and read scaling versus partitions for data distribution.
- Partition-key selection, skew, rebalancing, and cross-partition queries.
- Replication lag and read-after-write expectations.
- Failure detection, quorum concepts, and recovery behavior.

## Practice
Partition a growing activity dataset. Compare candidate keys and reason through skew, resharding, and a replica lagging behind.

## Ready when
You can describe the impact of a failed or overloaded partition and how data is redistributed or recovered.
