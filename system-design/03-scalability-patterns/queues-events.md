# Queues and Event-Driven Systems

## Learn
- Producers, brokers, consumers, partitions, and consumer groups.
- At-most-once, at-least-once, and effectively-once processing expectations.
- Idempotent consumers, retries, dead-letter queues, and poison messages.
- Event schemas, ordering scope, and backpressure.

## Practice
Move a slow side effect behind a queue. Explain delivery guarantees, duplicate handling, ordering, and how operators replay a failed message.

## Ready when
The system remains correct under duplicate delivery and queue growth is observable and actionable.
