# Load Balancing and Stateless Services

## Learn
- Horizontal scaling, load balancers, health checks, and service discovery.
- Stateless application instances and externalized session or durable state.
- Sticky sessions, locality, and when they complicate failover.
- Autoscaling signals, warm-up time, and capacity limits.

## Practice
Design a service that scales from one instance to many. Identify shared state, health criteria, and behavior during instance replacement.

## Ready when
Requests can move between instances without losing correctness and health checks reflect serving ability.
