# Consistency and Coordination

## Learn
- Strong, eventual, and session-level consistency in terms of user-visible behavior.
- Replication, clocks, concurrent writes, and conflict resolution.
- CAP as a constrained discussion of behavior during network partitions, not a database selector.
- Coordination cost, leader election concepts, and consensus at a high level.

## Practice
Define behavior for a user's profile read immediately after an update across regions. State what stale behavior is acceptable and how clients observe conflicts.

## Ready when
Consistency is described in user-visible guarantees rather than vague labels.
