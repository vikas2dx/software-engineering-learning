# Security and Privacy

## Learn
- Threat modeling, assets, actors, trust boundaries, and abuse cases.
- Authentication, authorization, least privilege, and service identity.
- Encryption in transit and at rest, key management, and secret rotation.
- Data minimization, retention, auditability, and secure logging.

## Practice
Threat-model a file-sharing or messaging system. Identify authorization checks, sensitive data stores, abuse controls, and audit events.

## Ready when
The design makes trust boundaries and enforcement points explicit and minimizes unnecessary sensitive data.
