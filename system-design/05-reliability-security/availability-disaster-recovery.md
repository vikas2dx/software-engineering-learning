# Availability and Disaster Recovery

## Learn
- Availability, durability, redundancy, and common-mode failures.
- Timeouts, bounded retries, circuit breakers, and graceful degradation.
- Backups, restore testing, recovery time objective, and recovery point objective.
- Multi-zone and multi-region tradeoffs, including operational complexity.

## Practice
Draw failure paths for a service and its database. Define what degrades, what stays available, and how restoration is verified.

## Ready when
Recovery goals are measurable and backed by tested procedures rather than architecture diagrams alone.
