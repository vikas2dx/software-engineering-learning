# Security and Privacy

## Learn
- App permissions, least privilege, and user consent.
- Secure storage, TLS, exported components, and intent validation.
- Do not embed backend secrets or log tokens and personal data.
- Data minimization, backups, retention, and dependency updates.

## Practice
Threat-model one feature from local storage to backend. Inspect manifest exposure, permission timing, and logs for sensitive values.

## Ready when
Trust boundaries are explicit and authorization is enforced by the server, not only by the app UI.
