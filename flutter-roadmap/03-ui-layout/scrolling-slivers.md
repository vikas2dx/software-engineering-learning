# Scrolling and Slivers

## Learn
- Lazy list construction and stable item identity.
- Scroll controllers, notifications, positions, and restoration.
- Slivers as composable scroll effects such as app bars, grids, and lists.
- Pagination, refresh, empty states, and preserving scroll position.

## Practice
Create a paginated feed with pull-to-refresh and a collapsing header. Ensure loading another page does not duplicate items.

## Ready when
You can compose scrollable regions without nested-scroll surprises and keep long lists efficient.
