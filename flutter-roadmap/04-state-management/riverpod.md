# Riverpod

## Learn
- Providers as declarations of dependencies and their state lifetimes.
- Watching, reading, and listening, and how each affects updates.
- Async providers, generated providers, overrides, and scoped dependencies.
- Keep business logic testable and model loading, data, and errors intentionally.

## Practice
Create an async repository provider and override it in a test. Verify refresh and error behavior at the UI boundary.

## Ready when
You can reason about provider recomputation and disposal, and choose providers that express dependencies clearly.
